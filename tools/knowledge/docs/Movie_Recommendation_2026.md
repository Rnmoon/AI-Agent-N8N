## 8\. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

How does the recommendation system work?

It analyzes selected genres, movie ratings, likes, and optionally viewing history to suggest relevant films.

Is the platform free to use?

The sample platform includes a free tier, with optional paid plans for additional features.

Can I search for a specific movie?

Yes. Users can search by movie title, genre, release year, actor, or director.

Can I save movies for later?

Yes. Users can create and manage a personal watchlist.

Can I rate and review movies?

Registered users can rate movies and submit reviews if the feature is enabled.

Does the platform stream movies?

Not necessarily. A recommendation platform helps users discover movies and may link to licensed streaming services. Streaming availability depends on the selected service and region.

How can I improve my recommendations?

Rate movies you have watched, update your preferred genres, and save titles you want to watch.

Can I change my preferences?

Yes. You can update preferred genres, languages, and other recommendation settings in your profile.
